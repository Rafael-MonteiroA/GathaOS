Gatha 8x16 bitmap font and Gatha cursor sprite
Original artwork created for this project, 2026.

The artwork is provided without warranty of any kind.

The 95 printable ASCII cells use original 5x7 drawings adapted to a six-pixel
body and nine-pixel cap height inside fixed 8x16 bitmap cells in Stage 8. Uppercase and digits extend
the project's original panic font. Lowercase and punctuation were drawn for
Stage 3. tools/make_font.py records every drawing; gui/gfx/font.h is generated
and checked in. No third-party font or cursor data was imported.
